# Appendix A: Glossary

> **Core Terminology Definitions for This Guide**

---

## A

**Active Perception**
- Agent actively selects perception content rather than passively receiving all information
- Includes: Selective attention, saccade control, goal-directed perception

**Agent**
- An autonomous entity capable of perceiving the environment, making decisions, and executing actions
- Core characteristics: Autonomy, reactivity, proactiveness

**Autonomy**
- The ability to self-determine and self-act
- Three elements: Independence, proactiveness, persistence

**Autonomous Agent**
- An agent that can operate without continuous external intervention
- Goal: From Level 0 (passive) to Level 4 (fully autonomous)

---

## B

**Belief State**
- Agent's perception of world state
- Contains: Uncertainty, confidence, probability distributions

**BuildBlock**
- Composable agent components
- Features: Unified interface, standardized, reusable

---

## C

**Constitutional AI**
- AI systems with built-in "constitutional" constraints
- Features: Inviolable principles, self-critique, automatic revision

**Curiosity**
- Pursuit of information gain
- Implementation: Surprise-driven, information gain-driven, novelty detection

**Collective Intelligence**
- Intelligence emerging from multi-agent systems
- Manifestations: Collaboration, division of labor, norms, culture

---

## D

**Decision System**
- Core module for agent action selection
- Types: Deliberative, reactive, autonomous, hybrid

**Deliberative Decision**
- Decision-making based on reasoning and planning
- Features: Deep thinking, forward simulation, optimal selection

**Dual Process Theory**
- Thinking divided into fast (System 1) and slow (System 2) systems
- Implication: Combine both decision modes in agent design

---

## E

**Embodiment**
- Agent has a "body" to interact with the environment
- Importance: Real causal learning, grounded cognition

**Episodic Memory**
- Stores specific events and experiences
- Features: Spatiotemporal markers, sensory details, autobiographical

**Event-Driven Loop**
- Runtime mode triggered by events
- Advantages: Fast response, resource-efficient
- Disadvantages: Passivity

**Exploration**
- Actively seeking information and new experiences
- Strategies: Random, epsilon-greedy, curiosity-driven

**Explainability**
- Agent behavior can be understood and explained
- Types: Post-hoc explanation, interpretable models, visualization

---

## F

**Free Energy Principle**
- Biological systems minimize free energy (surprise)
- Implication: Produces active exploration and action

**Fully Autonomous Agent**
- Level 4 autonomy, can autonomously define meaning of existence
- Current: Theoretical stage

---

## G

**Goal**
- State the agent desires to achieve
- Hierarchy: Meta-goals → Main goals → Sub-goals → Actions

**Goal Generation**
- Agent's ability to autonomously create goals
- Drivers: Environment, knowledge, values

**Generalization**
- Extracting abstract knowledge from specific experiences
- Methods: Induction, analogy, rule extraction

---

## H

**Hybrid Runtime**
- Combines event-driven, polling, and autonomous exploration
- Advantages: Balances responsiveness and proactiveness

**Hierarchical Decision**
- Strategy → Tactics → Operations → Execution
- Advantages: Handles complex long-term goals

---

## I

**Intrinsic Motivation**
- Drives originating from within the system
- Types: Curiosity, achievement, survival needs

**Information Gain**
- Knowledge increment from new information
- Calculation: KL divergence, entropy reduction

**Integrated Information Theory (IIT)**
- Mathematical theory of consciousness
- Core concept: Phi (integrated information)

---

## M

**Memory System**
- Mechanisms for agent to store and retrieve information
- Types: Working, episodic, semantic, metacognitive

**Metacognition**
- Cognition about one's own cognition and its control
- Components: Metacognitive knowledge, regulation, experience

**Meta-Learning**
- Learning how to learn
- Methods: MAML, Reptile, learning initialization

**Motivation System**
- Generates and regulates drives
- Components: Curiosity, achievement, survival needs

**Multimodal Fusion**
- Combining visual, auditory, text, and other information
- Methods: Early fusion, late fusion, attention fusion

---

## N

**Neural Correlates of Consciousness (NCC)**
- Neural correlates of consciousness
- Features: Global workspace, long-range synchronization, gamma band

**Novelty Detection**
- Identifying前所未见 information
- Methods: Similarity computation, density estimation

---

## O

**OpenClaw**
- Elegant agent deployment solution
- Features: One-click install, Docker Compose, CLI wizard

**Optimality**
- Decision is best under some standard
- Types: Pareto optimal, Nash equilibrium

---

## P

**Proactiveness**
- Not waiting for external triggers, actively seeking and acting
- Levels: Responsive proactive, exploratory proactive

**Polling Loop**
- Runtime mode that periodically checks status
- Advantages: Simple and reliable
- Disadvantages: Resource consumption

**Predictive Model**
- World model that predicts action consequences
- Uses: Planning, curiosity assessment, decision-making

---

## R

**ReAct Loop**
- Reasoning + Act loop
- Pattern: Thought → Action → Observation

**Reinforcement Learning**
- Learning optimal policies from reward signals
- Types: Value iteration, policy gradient, Actor-Critic

**Reflexive Decision**
- Fast decision based on stimulus-response
- Features: Unconscious, automatic, low latency

---

## S

**Semantic Memory**
- Stores knowledge and concepts
- Features: Abstract, decontextualized, propositional

**Self-Model**
- Agent's perception of its own capabilities
- Importance: Accurate self-assessment

**Swarm Intelligence**
- Collective intelligence from simple individuals
- Examples: Ant colonies, bee swarms, bird flocks

**Synthetic Motivation**
- Artificially designed intrinsic motivation systems
- Components: Curiosity + Achievement + Survival needs

---

## T

**Theory of Mind**
- Understanding that others have beliefs, desires, intentions
- Importance: Social collaboration, deception, communication

**Transfer Learning**
- Applying knowledge from one task to another
- Types: Inductive, instance-based, unsupervised

---

## V

**Value Alignment**
- Agent goals align with human values
- Methods: Constitutional AI, value learning, inverse reinforcement learning

**Visual Attention**
- Selectively processing visual information
- Types: Bottom-up (saliency), top-down (goal-directed)

---

## W

**Working Memory**
- Cache of currently attended information
- Capacity: Limited (~7 items)
- Function: Temporary storage, cognitive operations

**World Model**
- Agent's internal representation of the environment
- Uses: Prediction, planning, counterfactual reasoning

---

## Z

**Zero-Shot Learning**
- Handling new tasks without training
- Methods: Meta-learning, large model prompting
